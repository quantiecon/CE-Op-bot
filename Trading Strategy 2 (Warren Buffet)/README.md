# Trading Strategy 2 — Warren Buffett (Value Investing)

Alternative strategy framework that adopts Warren Buffett's value-investing
philosophy directly: buy high-quality, durable businesses at a reasonable
price and hold them for the long term.

## Files
- `TRADING-STRATEGY.md` — the rulebook (selection, valuation, position
  management, entry/exit checklists).
- `PHILOSOPHY.md` — the underlying philosophy: fundamental value, circle
  of competence, margin of safety, patient compounding.

## Relationship to Strategy 1
Strategy 1 (in `memory/TRADING-STRATEGY.md`) is a momentum-and-trailing-stop
framework. Strategy 2 is its philosophical opposite: concentration over
diversification, conviction over stops, patience over activity.

The two are not blended. Pick one regime and follow its rules; do not
cherry-pick across them mid-trade.
